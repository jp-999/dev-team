# AI Dev Team — Knowledge Vault

This folder is an **[Obsidian](https://obsidian.md/) vault**: the team's durable *company brain*.
Open it in Obsidian (*File → Open folder as vault*) for backlinks, tags, and the graph view — or
run `python3 scripts/vault_graph.py` for a no-Obsidian `Backlinks.md` + `graph.json`.

## Maps of content
- [[Roles]] — who's on the team
- [[Chapters]] — the craft axis (squads × chapters)
- [[Upskilling]] — improvements the L&D team adopts every 12 hours

## How it links
Notes connect with `[[wikilinks]]` and `#tags`. The automatic upskilling cycle writes one note per
improvement under `improvements/`, tagged by area and linked to the [[Chapters|chapter]] that owns
it — so the graph grows on its own every 12 hours.
