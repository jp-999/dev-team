# Upskilling

The [[researcher]]-led **L&D cycle runs automatically every 12 hours** (`workflow/upskilling.md`).
Each cycle the researcher scrapes the web, GitHub, and Medium; the team adopts one improvement and
rolls it out to every squad.

Each adopted improvement is written as a note under `improvements/`, tagged `#upskilling` plus its
area, and linked to the owning chapter in [[Chapters]]. Browse the growing set via the Obsidian
graph, or the generated [[Backlinks]] index.
